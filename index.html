Frequently Asked Questions

What are your hospital timings?

We are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for emergency services and admission. OPD timings vary by doctor.

Is emergency care available at night?

Yes, our emergency department and ICU are fully operational 24/7 with specialized doctors on duty.

Do I need an appointment for Panchakarma?

Yes, we recommend booking a consultation with our Ayurvedic experts to customize the Panchakarma therapy according to your specific needs.

How can I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment by filling out the form on this website, which will directly connect you to us via WhatsApp, or you can call us at 97280 30109.

Where is AAA Hospital located?
